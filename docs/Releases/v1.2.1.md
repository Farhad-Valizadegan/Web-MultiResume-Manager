## Changes

Public resume and generated PDF dates use abbreviated month and year, such as Feb 2023. Experience and education use month editing with server normalization and date-range checks. Future employment dates are rejected; ongoing entries and expected education completion are supported. Both editors use collapsible sections; fields remain mounted when closed and invalid fields are revealed.

Integrated work includes PR #43 (date presentation) and PR #44 (month editing and editor sections).
